Your question ladder
Read it as a progression: each rung is a different kind of thinking (Bloom's), and the depth (DOK) deepens as you climb. Lower rungs warm students up and surface what they know; the top rung is your target. The AI prompt below fills these in for your exact objective.
Two dials, moving together: Bloom's & DOK
Bloom's = the type of thinking
Remember → Understand → Apply → Analyze → Evaluate → Create. It names the cognitive operation a question asks for. It's the rung you stand on.
DOK = the depth of the thinking
DOK 1 recall · 2 a step of reasoning · 3 reason from evidence · 4 sustained investigation. It names how deep and complex the engagement is. It's how far down the rung goes.
They move together — but they're not the same axis
A good sequence usually climbs both at once. But you can hold a Bloom rung and deepen DOK (ask three "evaluate" questions, each deeper), or sit shallow at a high rung. The ladder shows the planned movement; flex it for your lesson.
